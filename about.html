About us

The house you walked to after school.

Before every snack lived in an app, a lot of neighborhoods had a candy lady. Not a chain. A person. The house you walked to after school with whatever was in your pocket.

The memory

That stop was simple. You knew the street. You knew what she carried. You didn't need a speech about community. You needed a cold drink and something sweet before you headed home. That memory is why this site exists: a marketplace that keeps the part people actually miss, a nearby sweets spot run by someone on your block.

What this is

The Candy Lady is a neighborhood candy and snack marketplace. Buyers shop candy, sweets, snacks, and drinks from local home-based sellers. You pay by card when you order and collect your bag with a pickup code. There's no delivery.

Sellers aren't random listings. A neighbor applies, the shop is reviewed, and it goes live only after approval. Each seller chooses what they carry, anything sweets-related, and sets their own prices. Sellers mark orders packing, ready, and complete so you know when to walk over.

Who it's for

If you want something sweet close to home, this is the shop window. If you already run a little candy setup, or you've always wanted to, this is the front door. Apply, get approved, stock your shop, and take pickup orders.

Who runs it

The Candy Lady is a sole proprietorship operating neighborhoodcandylady.com. It's an online neighborhood marketplace, not a walk-in candy store, and it isn't connected to other businesses that share the name. Questions: joshk93238@gmail.com. You can also visit the contact page.
