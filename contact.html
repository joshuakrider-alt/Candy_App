Contact

Write when you need a person.

The Candy Lady doesn't have a shop phone or a public storefront. Email is the way to reach the person who runs the site.

Email The Candy Ladyjoshk93238@gmail.com

To help us help you, include:

  • Whether you're a buyer or a seller.
  • The shop name, if you have one.
  • Your pickup code, if it's about an order.

Good reasons to write:

  • Help with an account or order.
  • A question about a seller application.
  • Pausing or closing your shop.
  • A privacy or deletion request.
  • Something on the site that looks wrong.

About pickup

Pickup happens at each seller's shop, not a central counter, so check that shop's pickup notes first.
